# SIH 26033: Multiple Intermediaries Reduce Farmers' Earnings and Increase Consumer Prices

## 1. Proposed Solution

Our idea is to develop an online platform that connects farmers directly with consumers, retailers, restaurants, and bulk buyers. At present, farmers often depend on several middlemen to sell their products. Because of this, farmers may not receive a fair price, while consumers end up paying more.

Through our platform, farmers can list their agricultural products, mention the available quantity, and set their expected prices. Buyers can view the products, compare prices, and place orders directly with farmers.

We also plan to include a logistics facility to help transport products from farms to buyers. AI will be used to predict product demand and suggest better delivery routes. This will help farmers sell their products more easily and reduce unnecessary costs in the supply chain.

## 2. Technical Approach

We plan to develop this platform using the following technologies:

- **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript and React.js to create a simple and user-friendly website.
- **Backend:** Python with Django or Flask to manage the platform and its operations.
- **Database:** MySQL or PostgreSQL to store farmer details, product information, orders, and customer details.
- **Artificial Intelligence:** Machine Learning to predict demand for agricultural products and help farmers plan their sales.
- **Maps and Logistics:** Map APIs to identify locations, calculate distances, and find suitable delivery routes.
- **Payment Gateway:** To support secure online payments between buyers and sellers.

### Implementation Process

1. First, we will create separate registration and login options for farmers, consumers, and bulk buyers.
2. Farmers will be able to upload their products along with prices, quantities, and other details.
3. Buyers can browse the available products and order directly from farmers.
4. A logistics system will help arrange transportation and delivery.
5. AI-based demand prediction will help farmers understand which products are likely to have higher demand.
6. Route optimization will help reduce delivery time and transportation costs.
7. Finally, we will test the platform and make improvements based on feedback from users.

## 3. Feasibility and Viability

This project is possible to develop using currently available technologies such as web development frameworks, databases, AI models, and map services.

We plan to start with a small group of farmers or FPOs in a particular area and gradually expand the platform to other regions.

### Challenges We May Face

- Some farmers may not be familiar with using digital platforms.
- Arranging transportation between different locations may be difficult.
- Product availability and prices may change frequently.
- Building trust between farmers and buyers may take time.
- Maintaining product quality during transportation can be challenging.

### How We Plan to Overcome These Challenges

- Keep the platform simple and easy to use, with support for regional languages.
- Work with local FPOs to help farmers understand and use the platform.
- Connect with existing transportation and delivery services.
- Display clear product details, prices, and order information.
- Introduce customer reviews and product quality checks.

In the future, the platform can generate revenue through a small service fee on successful orders and optional services for bulk buyers. However, our main focus will be to keep the platform affordable for farmers.

## 4. Impact and Benefits

### Benefits for Farmers
- Farmers can sell their products directly to buyers.
- They can have better control over their selling prices.
- They can reach more customers without depending heavily on middlemen.
- Demand predictions can help them plan their sales.

### Benefits for Consumers
- Consumers can get agricultural products at more reasonable prices.
- They can compare products and prices before purchasing.
- They can get better information about where their products come from.

### Benefits for the Agricultural Sector
- Reduces unnecessary intermediaries in the supply chain.
- Helps improve the connection between farmers and buyers.
- Creates new opportunities for local delivery and logistics services.
- Helps reduce food wastage through better demand planning.
- Optimized delivery routes can reduce fuel consumption and transportation costs.

Overall, our goal is to make agricultural product selling and purchasing easier, fairer, and more transparent for everyone involved.
